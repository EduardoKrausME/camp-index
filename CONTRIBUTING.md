# Contributing to the camp index

Most contributions here are claim pull requests and entry edits; the
how-to lives in
[AUTHORS.md](https://github.com/camp-registry/camp-docs/blob/main/AUTHORS.md).
Issues are the right place for seed requests, removal requests, and
anything else — templates exist for the common cases.

## The licensing term

By contributing to the index (claim PRs, entry edits, release records,
advisory corrections), you dedicate your contribution to the public
domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Why: index contributions are small factual statements (who maintains a
plugin, where it lives, what was released). Accepting them under CC0
lets the registry publish the assembled database under its data
license (currently ODbL; advisories CC BY 4.0 — see
[LICENSE-DATA.md](LICENSE-DATA.md)) and adjust that license in the
future as a single party, without tracking down every past contributor
for consent.

What this does NOT touch: your plugin's code, your `.camp/listing.yml`
content, and your artifacts stay under your plugin's own license,
always. The CC0 grant covers only your edits to this index repository.
